06 · Get started

Wake him up

  1. Paste this in TerminalIt takes about a minute and adds a birch command.
  2. Finish setup in your browserStar the repo and follow @veds260 to unlock it, then Birch downloads ffmpeg, whisper and its speech model with one button each.
  3. Drop in a clipOr run birch cut talk.mov, or ask Claude or ChatGPT to cut it for you.
